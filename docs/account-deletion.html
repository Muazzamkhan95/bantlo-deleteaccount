Request account & data deletion

Use this page to ask Codebeams to delete your Bantlo account and the personal data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap or click the button below to open an email to muazzamkhan95@gmail.com.
  2. Keep the subject line, and include the email address used for your Bantlo account (and Google Sign-In email if different).
  3. Send the message. We verify ownership of the account before deleting anything.

Email deletion request

If the button does not open your mail app, send an email yourself to muazzamkhan95@gmail.com with the subject “Bantlo account deletion request”.

What we delete

After verifying your request, we permanently delete:

  • Your Bantlo account and authentication credentials
  • Profile details (name, email, photo URL)
  • Group memberships and related account preferences
  • Expenses, settlements, comments, and activity you created, where they are not still needed for other members’ shared group records
  • Device notification tokens and local PIN credentials tied to you

What may be retained

  • Shared group content that other members still need (for example, an expense you added in an active group may remain visible to them, with your name removed or anonymized where practical)
  • Records we must keep for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting reasons, for as long as required by law
  • Aggregated analytics that cannot identify you

Timing

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email when the process is finished.

Deletion is permanent. If you have an active Bantlo Pro subscription, cancel it in Google Play before or when requesting deletion.
